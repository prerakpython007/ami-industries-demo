FAQs

Frequently Asked
Questions

What are the primary differences between conveyor ovens and batch ovens?
+
Conveyor ovens are ideal for continuous production processes, where materials move through the oven on a conveyor belt — consistent heating over a longer duration. Batch ovens are suited for processing materials in discrete batches, making them suitable for applications requiring flexibility in load sizes and variations in processing times.
What is the difference between AMI Industries and a local oven manufacturer?
+
AMI Industries prioritizes quality control with advanced manufacturing and rigorous testing. Our ovens feature superior air circulation designs for consistent temperatures. We conduct thorough heat load calculations for each application and specialize in fully custom solutions. With 45+ years of experience, 6,500+ installations, and 50+ MNC clients, we offer unparalleled expertise that local manufacturers simply cannot match.
When should I choose electric heating vs. gas-fired vs. IR vs. UV?
+
Electric Heating: Ideal for precise temperature control and cleanliness, where gas is impractical. Gas-Fired: More energy-efficient for large-scale operations needing high temperatures and fast heating. Infrared (IR): Best for rapid heating — fast heat transfer directly to the material for drying and curing. UV: Ideal for curing coatings and inks, fast curing times with low heat output.
What temperature range do AMI ovens cover?
+
AMI Industries covers the full range from 50°C to 1150°C across all our product categories. This covers everything from gentle food drying at ambient temperatures through to high-temperature furnace work for special alloys, glass and ceramic processing.
Do you offer custom solutions?
+
Absolutely — at AMI Industries, we firmly believe that no two clients have the same requirements. Every oven, dryer, or furnace we build is engineered specifically for the client's process, product, space, and production requirements. We do not believe in a one-size-fits-all approach. From initial technical discussion to 3D drawing preparation, from fabrication to on-site installation and commissioning — every step is handled with precision.
What maintenance do industrial ovens require?
+
Routine inspections of heating elements and controls, cleaning filters and ventilation systems, checking insulation and seals for wear, calibration of temperature controls, and addressing operational issues promptly. AMI provides complete technical documentation and wiring diagrams with every oven for easy maintenance, plus our team is always available for after-sales support.
What is the typical lead time for orders?
+
Lead times vary based on product specifications and order size. Standard products have shorter lead times, while custom-built large systems and turnkey plants require longer lead times. We will provide an estimated delivery date upon order confirmation. AMI is committed to timely delivery — we honour our deadlines every time.
Can AMI assist with installation and training?
+
Absolutely! AMI Industries provides comprehensive on-site installation services, commissioning, and operator training for all our products. Our experienced engineers ensure your staff is well-equipped to operate and maintain the equipment effectively, maximizing your investment. After-sales support is always available.
